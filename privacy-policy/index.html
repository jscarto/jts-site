Jason’s Tech Services, LLC (“JTS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information obtained through getjts.com, our contact forms, telephone calls, text messages, emails, and customer service interactions.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Messages and service inquiries
  • Service or installation address
  • Installation requirements and property details
  • Information necessary to prepare estimates, schedule appointments, provide services, or support completed installations

Our website provider and security services may automatically receive limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This information may be used to operate, secure, and improve the website.

How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to questions and service requests
  • Prepare estimates and recommendations
  • Schedule and complete installations or service appointments
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate about requested or existing services
  • Coordinate services with a vendor when necessary to fulfill your request
  • Maintain business and service records
  • Protect our website and business against fraud, misuse, or security threats
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

How We Share Information

Jason’s Tech Services does not sell or rent personal information. We do not share customer information with unrelated companies for their independent marketing purposes.

We may provide limited information to vendors or service providers when reasonably necessary to fulfill a service requested by the customer. These may include internet-service providers, equipment suppliers, website and email providers, or other businesses involved in completing the requested service.

We may also disclose information when required by law, court order, governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect our customers, business, property, or legal rights.

Mobile telephone information and text-message consent will not be sold or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Telephone, Text, and Email Communications

When you provide contact information, JTS may contact you by telephone, text message, or email to respond to your inquiry, provide an estimate, schedule service, coordinate an installation, or provide customer support.

Message and data rates may apply to text messages. You may ask us to stop promotional text messages by replying STOP. You may request help by replying HELP or contacting us directly. Opting out of promotional communications will not prevent necessary service-related communications.

Cookies and Website Services

Our website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technology required for website operation, security, performance, and fraud prevention.

Our website uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam and abuse. Information processed by reCAPTCHA is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Information Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information. However, no website, email system, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, meet warranty or support obligations, and comply with legal requirements.

Your Choices

You may contact us to:

  • Ask what personal information we have about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information, subject to legitimate business and legal recordkeeping requirements
  • Opt out of promotional communications

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to internet providers, equipment manufacturers, or other third-party websites. JTS is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independently operated third-party websites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
